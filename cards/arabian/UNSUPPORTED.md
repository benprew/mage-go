# Arabian Nights — Unsupported Cards

Cards registered as no-op stubs because their mechanics cannot be implemented
in the current engine. Marked `UNIMPLEMENTABLE` in source.

| Card | Reason |
|------|--------|
| Shahrazad | Subgame mechanic — requires recursive game instances. Banned in all sanctioned formats. |
| Ring of Ma'ruf | Wish/sideboard — requires access to cards outside the game. |
